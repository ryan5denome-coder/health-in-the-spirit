Every week we get the same messages. A question about a supplement. A follow-up on something we said in an episode. A parent asking what they would actually do in our house. The show is one direction. This is meant to be the other one.

## What it will be

**Weekly member Q&A.** Ryan and Annie, live, answering the questions you bring. Not a webinar. A conversation, with a chiropractor and a theologian in the room.

**Ask the doc.** A running thread where you can put a question in front of Ryan and get a real answer instead of a search engine's guess. Not a diagnosis, and never a substitute for your own doctor. A straight answer from someone who does this for a living.

**Seasonal challenges.** Health practices that follow the Christian calendar instead of fighting it. Fasting that means something in Lent. Feasting that means something at Easter. Ordinary time for the slow, unglamorous work of building a body that lasts.

**Downloadable protocols.** The handouts. Sleep, gut, movement, food. What we actually give patients and what we actually do at home, written down so you can start on a Monday.

**Bonus episodes.** Conversations that ran long, topics that were too specific for the main feed, and the occasional thing we would only say to people who have already been listening for a while.

## Where it stands

Early. Honestly early. The show came first and the community is being built behind it, deliberately, so that the first hundred people get something worth their time instead of an empty forum. We would rather open late than open thin.
