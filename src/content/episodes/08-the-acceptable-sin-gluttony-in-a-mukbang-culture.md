<p>There's a sin most of us will never confess — and half the time we're celebrating it in the parish hall. Ryan and Annie take on gluttony: not the cartoon version with the Roman emperor, but the version that looks like a chocolate croissant every stressful afternoon, or the show you can't wait to get to once the kids are down. The question underneath it isn't <em>how much did you eat</em>. It's <strong>who's in charge</strong>.</p><p>Annie tells what happened the first time she went no-sugar — the yeast overgrowth and parasite that were driving the cravings, and the part she didn't expect: that stripping out the sugar left her feeling <em>stripped</em>, and showed her exactly how much comfort she'd been getting from the next treat instead of from God.
