The answer was no — or rather, not yet. This is the episode where she explains why she's grateful for that. Starting around age ten she dealt with severe acne, bloating so bad she looked six months pregnant, a personal record of twenty-one days without a bowel movement, painful cycles, and a brain fog that made college feel like wading through mud. She saw allergists and dermatologists, tried every cream and cleanser and elimination diet, and got nowhere — until comprehensive lab work found what nobody had thought to look for. God isn't a symptom God, she says.
