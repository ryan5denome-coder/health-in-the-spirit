<p>Dom Valenti wanted to be a chiropractor. At sixteen, a radiologist talked him out of it. Years later an academic advisor looked at a C+ in zoology and told him he'd never make it to med school. Both were doors closing — and Dom now says both were God. He spent four years in a busy emergency room and works today in a cardiac ICU, and in this conversation he tells Ryan what he's actually seen: not lightning-bolt healings on the hospital floor, but what he calls micro-miracles. A 36-year-old stroke patient who hadn't been out of bed in seven days. A bath, a beard trim, some music, and a man fist-bumping the air from the edge of the mattress.
