<p>Ryan and Annie have one scripture passage they keep coming back to — the one they've built a marriage, a clinic, and this show around. It's Matthew 25:14–30, and most of the time you hear it when someone at church is about to ask for money. They want to take it somewhere else. Their reading comes down to a simple equation: your affinity (the gifts you've actually been given, the direction your heart already leans) multiplied by your capacity (not what you think you can carry, but the bandwidth God measured for you) equals your talents. Multiply that by cultivation and you get your return.
