Two years on, he threw a pitch that rolled halfway to home plate, and an MRI came back showing a bilateral fracture through his L5 — both of the little bridges of bone that hold the vertebra in place, snapped. No baseball. No running. No swimming. Six months to a year if he healed well. What happened over the next three months — a retreat he was dragged onto, a night of adoration, a healing service in Indiana, and a second MRI — is the reason he's a chiropractor today.
