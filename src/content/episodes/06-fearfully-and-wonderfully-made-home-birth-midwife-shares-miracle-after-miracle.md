<p>Ryan and Annie interviewed a lot of midwives before their home birth. Every one of them ran through the schedule — first visit at 20 weeks, this at 22, this at 30. Hannah Penaluna did almost none of that. She talked about how she'd walk with them, and about inviting the Holy Spirit into it. This episode was recorded at her kitchen table outside Atlanta, and it covers a lot of ground: seven years of hospital midwifery in Australia, including a remote posting in the Torres Strait where she cared for a population with heart disease, diabetes and deep trauma; why 2021 made staying impossible; and the resuscitation she'll never forget — a baby with meconium aspiration who took no breath
